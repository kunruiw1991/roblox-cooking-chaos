# Cooking Chaos (Hot Wok Studios)

Play live: https://kunruiw1991.github.io/roblox-cooking-chaos/

A 3D isometric Roblox-style restaurant kitchen action & RNG chef-class game built with Three.js r170 and pure procedural WebAudio.

## Gameplay

- **Receive & Complete Orders**: Watch the top-left Order Queue for **Sushi Roll** (`Cucumber + Steamed Rice + Seaweed`), **Tuna Roll** (`Tuna + Steamed Rice + Seaweed`), **Chef Burger** (`Grilled Patty + Bun`), and **Sashimi Plate** (`Cucumber + Seared Tuna`).
- **Prep, Cook & Plate**:
  - Grab raw ingredients from the **North Wall** crates.
  - Slice cucumbers and tuna on the **West & Center Butcher-Block Cutting Boards** with animated chopping cleavers.
  - Boil rice and sear patties or fish on the **East Wall Gas Cooktop Stoves**.
  - Combine ingredients onto clean plates on the **South Wall Plating Counter** (or directly onto a plate in your hands) and drop completed dishes onto the **Red Motorized Serving Conveyor Belt**!
- **Extinguish Kitchen Fires**: Leave cooked food unattended on a hot burner for over 6 seconds and it will smoke and burst into 3D blocky fire! Grab the **Red Fire Extinguisher** from the South-West wall mount and spray white foam to douse the flames.
- **RNG Chef Class Gacha Wheel**: Between shifts (or anytime from the topbar Class badge), spin the **Chef Class Gacha Wheel** to unlock and equip perks (**Novice Chopper**, **Dash Runner**, **Tip Saver**, **Chop Master**, **Sharp Slice**, **Fireproof Pro**) with an optional `+30% LUCK` boost.

## Controls

### iPad & Touch Devices (Auto-Detected)
- **Left Translucent Thumbstick**: Fixed isometric walking (zero camera spin).
- **Green ACTION Button** (bottom-right): Dynamic context action (`GET`, `CHOP`, `COOK`, `PLATE`, `SERVE!`, `GRAB EXTINGUISHER`, `SPRAY FIRE!`).
- **Blue DASH Button**: Quick burst sprint across the kitchen.
- **1-Tap Station Assist**: Tap any kitchen crate, cutting board, stove, or counter to walk right up and interact.

### Keyboard & Mouse
- **WASD / Arrow Keys**: Move chef across the kitchen.
- **E / Space**: Pick up, put down, plate, or serve dishes.
- **F**: Chop faster at cutting boards or spray the Fire Extinguisher.
- **Shift**: Quick dash sprint.
- **Esc**: Open/close menu.
